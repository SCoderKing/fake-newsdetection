📚 Media Literacy Tips

Use these simple steps when you receive suspicious information online.

1️⃣

Check the Source

Find out who published the information and whether the source is identifiable.

2️⃣

Check the Date

Old news can sometimes be shared as if it were a recent event.

3️⃣

Read Beyond the Headline

Headlines may not always provide the complete context.

4️⃣

Cross-Check

Compare important claims with multiple reliable sources.

5️⃣

Check Images

Images can be old, edited or used with a different context.

6️⃣

Think Before Sharing

If you are not sure, avoid forwarding the information.
